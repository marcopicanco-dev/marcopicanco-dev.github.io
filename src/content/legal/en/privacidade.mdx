## Summary

- The site has no sign-up, forms or login area.
- Google Analytics is only enabled if you accept it in the cookie notice. You can change your choice at any time under “Cookie preferences” in the footer.
- Comments on articles are made with your GitHub account and are public.
- I do not sell data, I do not use data for advertising and I do not make automated decisions about you.

The data controller is **Marco Picanço**, who runs the site [www.marcopicanco.com](https://www.marcopicanco.com/). ### Visiting the site

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages) and served through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/), which delivers the pages, blocks malicious traffic and hides email addresses from bots. This processing happens on every visit, because it is what brings the site to you securely.

Performance metrics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or local storage and does not identify individual visitors.

### Analytics with Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 only loads after you click “Accept” or turn on “Analytics” in the preferences. It is configured without Google signals and without ad personalization, and Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. If you withdraw your consent, the site stops sending data and deletes the `_ga` cookies from this browser.

### Comments

Comments use [giscus](https://giscus.app/), an open source service that stores each conversation in the [discussions of a public GitHub repository](https://github.com/marcopicanco-dev/giscus/discussions). When you scroll down to the comments, your browser connects to giscus to show the conversation. To comment or react, you sign in with your GitHub account, and what you publish is visible to anyone, along with your username and photo.

You can edit or delete your comments directly on GitHub. I may hide or remove comments that break the [Terms of Use](/en/termos/).

Necessary items do not depend on consent, because without them the site does not work as expected or is left unprotected. Analytics items are only stored with your permission. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.

## Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you can request:

- confirmation that I process your data and access to it;
- correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
- anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed in breach of the law;
- data portability;
- deletion of data processed based on your consent;
- information about who your data has been shared with;
- information about the possibility of not giving consent and what happens if you do not;
- withdrawal of consent, at any time;
- objection to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of them, write to [marcopicanco@live.com](mailto:marcopicanco@live.com). I reply within 15 days. To withdraw consent for analytics, just use “Cookie preferences” in the footer. You can also file a complaint with Brazil’s [National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br).

## Security

The site is static and has no database of its own: I do not store sign-ups or messages on my own servers. Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS with HSTS), and a content security policy (CSP) restricts which scripts and services can run on the pages. If a security incident occurs that may cause you significant risk or harm, I will notify the affected data subjects and the ANPD, as required by art. 48 of the LGPD.

## Children and teenagers

The site is not aimed at children. Commenting requires a GitHub account, which is only available to people aged 13 and over.

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change affects what is collected based on your consent, the cookie notice will appear again so you can decide.
